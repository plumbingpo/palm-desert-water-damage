Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Palm Desert Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Palm Desert, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

Why Hire a Professional for Sewage Cleanup?

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Dark residue from sewage left on the basement floor or ringing a drain
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape
  • Address slow drains promptly, before they become complete blockages
